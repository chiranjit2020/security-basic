# Foundations

Before the security sequence (CIA → AuthN/AuthZ → Cryptography → …), there is one model
worth having in place: **the lifecycle of your data**.

Most learning paths are organized around *technologies* — TCP/IP, RAM, MySQL, TLS, backups.
The states-of-data model is the higher-level classification that connects them, and every
later chapter leans on it.

- [The Three States of Data](states-of-data.md) — at rest, in use, in transit.
- [Backup Data](backup-data.md) — where backups fit, and why they are not a fourth state.
